## What Is EmailBuddy?

EmailBuddy is a native iPhone app that uses artificial intelligence to clean up your Gmail or Outlook inbox fast. It connects to your email account using secure OAuth, scans your inbox, classifies every sender using AI, and lets you bulk-delete, archive, or unsubscribe from thousands of emails in minutes.

If you have 5,000 unread emails and do not know where to start, EmailBuddy is built for exactly that problem. It groups your inbox by sender, tells you which ones are newsletters, promotions, or junk, and lets you trash entire categories in one tap.

EmailBuddy is not a general email client. It does not replace Gmail or Outlook for reading and sending. It is a dedicated inbox cleaning tool, built from the ground up for the task of getting to inbox zero on iPhone.

## Core Features

### AI Sender Classification

EmailBuddy uses GPT-4o-mini to analyze your inbox and classify every sender into categories:

- **Newsletters** — content subscriptions, blogs, publications
- **Promotions** — retail, deals, discount emails
- **Social notifications** — Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram alerts
- **Transactional** — receipts, confirmations, shipping updates
- **Low priority** — automated notifications, alerts, reminders
- **Important** — personal emails, replies, flagged senders

This classification happens automatically when you open the app. You do not need to manually label anything.

### Bulk Cleanup Actions

Once your inbox is classified, you can take bulk actions on entire sender groups or categories:

- **Trash** — move all emails from a sender to trash
- **Archive** — move to archive, out of the inbox
- **Mark as read** — clear unread counts in bulk
- **Delete permanently** — skip trash and delete immediately

You can clean up hundreds or thousands of emails from a single sender in one tap.

### Inbox Health Score

EmailBuddy calculates a real-time inbox health score based on:

- Number of unread emails
- Percentage of emails from low-value senders
- Proportion of newsletters vs. important email
- Cleanup activity over time

The score goes from 0 to 100. As you clean up your inbox, the score improves. You can track your progress over days and weeks.

### One-Tap Unsubscribe

For newsletter senders, EmailBuddy detects the unsubscribe link in the email headers and handles the unsubscribe process for you. You do not need to open each email and find the unsubscribe button manually.

### Inbox Summary (AI)

EmailBuddy can generate an AI summary of what is in your inbox right now: which senders have the most emails, what categories dominate your inbox, and what actions would have the biggest cleanup impact.

### Multi-Account Support

EmailBuddy supports linking multiple Gmail accounts. Pro users can manage up to 3 Gmail accounts from a single app.

### Push Notifications

EmailBuddy can send push notifications for inbox insights and weekly cleanup reminders, helping you stay on top of your inbox over time.

## How EmailBuddy Works — Step by Step

1. Download EmailBuddy from the App Store (free)
2. Create an account using Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or email and password
3. Connect your Gmail or Outlook account using OAuth (no password required)
4. EmailBuddy scans your inbox and groups emails by sender
5. AI classifies each sender — you see newsletters, promotions, and junk highlighted
6. Tap a sender or category and choose: Trash, Archive, or Mark as Read
7. Confirm the action — EmailBuddy executes it via the Gmail or Outlook API
8. Your inbox health score updates in real time

The entire setup takes under 2 minutes. Most users clean up thousands of emails in their first session.

## Privacy and Security

EmailBuddy is designed with privacy as a core principle, not an afterthought.

- **OAuth only** — EmailBuddy never asks for your email password. It connects using Google OAuth 2.0 and Microsoft OAuth 2.0, the same standard used by all major apps.
- **No email storage** — your email content is never saved to EmailBuddy's servers. Emails are read in real time via the Gmail or Outlook API and processed in memory only.
- **iOS Keychain encryption** — OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain (same security layer used by banking apps). They are never stored in plain text.
- **Google-verified** — EmailBuddy's Gmail integration has been reviewed and approved by Google for the gmail.modify, gmail.readonly, and gmail.send OAuth scopes. CASA Tier 2 security assessment completed.
- **Minimal data collection** — EmailBuddy collects only what is necessary: your email address for account creation, subscription status, and aggregate inbox statistics (not email content).
- **Data retention** — inbox metrics are automatically deleted after 90 days. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

## Comparison to Other Inbox Tools

### EmailBuddy vs. Gmail App (native)
The native Gmail app is excellent for reading and sending email. It has no bulk cleanup tools, no AI classification, and no inbox health tracking. EmailBuddy is not a replacement for Gmail — it is a companion tool for cleaning.

### EmailBuddy vs. Clean Email
Clean Email is a web-based inbox management tool. EmailBuddy is a native iOS app with no browser required. EmailBuddy uses on-device AI classification and does not require you to grant access to a web service dashboard.

### EmailBuddy vs. Unroll.me
Unroll.me was widely criticized for selling anonymized user email data to third parties (including Uber). EmailBuddy does not sell or share any user data. Email content is never stored on EmailBuddy servers.

### EmailBuddy vs. SaneBox
SaneBox is primarily a filtering tool for future emails. It learns what is important and files the rest. EmailBuddy is focused on cleaning up your existing inbox backlog, not filtering incoming mail.

### EmailBuddy vs. Mailstrom
Mailstrom is browser-based and desktop-first. EmailBuddy is built for iPhone and works entirely from the iOS app, no browser login required.

### EmailBuddy vs. Spark
Spark is a full email client for reading, sending, and organizing. EmailBuddy is not a general email client. It is a dedicated cleaning tool.
